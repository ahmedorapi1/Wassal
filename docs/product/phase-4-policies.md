# Phase 4 product and policy notes

## Delivery evidence

WASSAL does not collect electronic proof of delivery in Phase 4. A merchant may
use a paper sheet outside the application containing the daily sequence,
readable WASSAL order number, recipient details, date, handwritten signature,
and notes. The paper is optional external evidence that operations may inspect
manually during a dispute; it is not uploaded, scanned, or validated.

## Delivery and money

The merchant has 24 hours after the recorded delivery instant to dispute by
default. That exact deadline is snapshotted on the order. Commission becomes
due only after an undisputed deadline or an administrator’s resolution.
Courier-uploaded payment proof does not reduce liability until finance approval.

## Location

Google Maps links are accepted only as a convenient source of explicit
coordinates and are parsed locally. Distance and duration are approximate,
offline estimates. The courier chooses external navigation. There is no live
tracking, foreground/background GPS capture, or paid directions API.

## Pilot access

Courier and merchant registrations are pending until administrative approval.
Phone/password is intentionally limited to the controlled pilot. Public launch
requires verified SMS OTP for courier/merchant signup, recovery and sensitive
phone changes, provider monitoring/failover, anti-abuse, and consent evidence.
Customers have neither accounts nor OTP.

## Communications and payments

Notifications are in-app only. WASSAL does not send SMS or WhatsApp in this
phase. External courier payments remain cash/bank/mobile-wallet evidence
reviewed by finance; WASSAL does not integrate a payment gateway or automated
payout.

The public Arabic-first privacy and terms pages live at `/privacy` and `/terms`
in the merchant web application and include English summaries. Both are marked
as drafts and must be reviewed by a qualified Egyptian lawyer before launch.
